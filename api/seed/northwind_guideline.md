Northwind Tea Co. Brand Guidelines

1. Voice and tone
Our voice is calm, precise, understated and quietly knowledgeable.
1.1 Write in a calm, precise voice. No hype, no slang and no corporate jargon.
1.3 Never use exclamation marks and never use all-caps for emphasis.
Do: "Our leaves are picked by hand in the first flush."
Do: "Steep for three minutes and notice the colour change."
Don't: "OMG the BEST tea you will EVER drink!!!"
Don't: "Unlock a game-changing infusion experience."

2. Terminology
2.1 Say "tea leaves", never "tea bags" or "cuppa". We sell whole leaf tea, and slang is off-brand.
2.2 Say "guests", not "customers" or "consumers".
2.4 Say "estate-grown", never "cheap tea" or "bargain tea". Never frame origin around price.

4. Claims
4.1 Only make product claims listed in the approved claims register. No unqualified superlatives.
Approved claims:
- Our leaves are picked by hand on two family estates in Darjeeling (Estate Report 2025, p. 3)
- Every tin is plastic-free and fully recyclable (Packaging Brief, p. 1)
- Packed within 72 hours of the final drying (Operations Handbook, p. 8)
